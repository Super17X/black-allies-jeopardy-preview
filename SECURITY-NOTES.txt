For authenticated anti-spoofing, use Supabase private channels with per-user Realtime authorization, a verified host role, and room membership rules; enforce scoring and winner decisions at a trusted authority. Public room names, browser-generated IDs and frontend limits are not authentication. Current package deliberately preserves existing Supabase settings. Official reference: https://supabase.com/docs/guides/realtime/authorization

Session upgrade: ordinary joins and player commands are checked against a persistent random browser-device token, and room locking/removal are enforced by the board. Tokens are restored with the board snapshot. These controls prevent accidental duplicate-name claims and ordinary unregistered commands. They are not cryptographic authentication: this public broadcast relay exposes messages to room participants, and a modified client can observe/replay tokens or impersonate host messages. A customer deployment requiring adversarial protection needs authenticated private channels and server-enforced membership/host authority. No backend authorization policies were changed in this update.

Recovery files include secret wagers, answers, and device tokens. Keep them with the host; do not distribute them to players. Browser recovery is local to the board's room and origin. Clearing browser storage removes it. Export before events; import using the same room link on a replacement board. Recovery resumes clocks from saved time rather than counting offline time. Do not open two active board tabs for the same room.
